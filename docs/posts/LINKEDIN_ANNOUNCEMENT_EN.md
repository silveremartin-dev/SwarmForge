## 🐜 Post Copy

🚀 **Excited to announce the official release of SwarmForge v2.0 — An Open-Source High-Performance Eusocial Insect Simulation & Computational Biology Platform!** 🐜🐝🪵

Over the last several years, modeling complex collective intelligence and emergent behaviors in social insect colonies (ants, bees, wasps, termites) has required bridging microscopic ethology with massive multi-agent computational scale.

Today, we are launching **SwarmForge v2.0** alongside **autonomous, standalone 1-click packages** (no Java/Maven installation required).

### 🔍 Key Engineering & Biological Highlights:

🔹 **100% Data-Driven Ecological Modeling**: Zero hardcoded constants. Morphological parameters, developmental staging, mandibular forces (MPa), Arrhenius $Q_{10} = 2.2$ thermal kinetics, and caste ratios are driven dynamically by structured biological presets.
🔹 **Artemis-odb ECS Architecture**: Structure-of-Arrays memory allocation, custom 256-bit bitmask ethology covering 220+ social behaviors, and sub-millisecond $O(1)$ spatial queries.
🔹 **Physics-Based Nest Thermodynamics**: Real-time stack-effect buoyancy ventilation, 1D Fourier thermal conduction, and metabolic $CO_2$ dispersion grids.
🔹 **GPU Pheromone Acceleration**: OpenCL / TornadoVM compute kernels for real-time 3D trail diffusion, evaporation, and chemotactic gradients.
🔹 **Multi-Tier Cognitive Systems**: Integration of Belief-Desire-Intention (BDI) architectures, multi-rate FSMs, and PyTorch ONNX runtime bridging.
🔹 **Distributed Megaterrarium Sharding**: Multi-node cluster coordination via gRPC with real-time border migration and boundary halo synchronizers.
